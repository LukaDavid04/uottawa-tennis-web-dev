Donations


Support UOttawa Varsity Tennis!


Your generous donations make a real difference to our varsity tennis program!

Throughout the fall season, our men’s and women’s teams travel across Ontario to compete against universities in the OUA. These trips come with significant expenses, including transportation, gas and hotel accommodations.

Beyond competition, our athletes train year-round and work hard to represent the University of Ottawa at the highest level. Donations also help cover essential program expenses, including OUA participation fees, training and other team-related costs.

Every contribution, big or small, helps us continue building a competitive program and providing our student-athletes with opportunities to grow, compete and succeed.

Thank you for supporting UOttawa Varsity Tennis!
